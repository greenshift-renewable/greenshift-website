One team, the full project lifecycle.

Most solar installations fail at the handoffs — between the company that designs the system, the one that supplies it, and whoever eventually maintains it. Greenshift runs all four stages under one roof.

01 — Design

Site survey & system design

Load assessment, roof/site survey, and sizing for solar PV, hybrid, or battery-storage systems matched to actual consumption.

02 — Supply

Equipment sourcing

Panels, inverters, LiFePO4 batteries, and balance-of-system components sourced and specified for the design.

03 — Install

Installation & commissioning

Mounting, wiring, and testing carried out to standard, with full system commissioning before handover.

04 — Maintain

Ongoing maintenance

Ongoing inspection and battery health monitoring to keep systems performing well after installation.

Deep battery and electrical expertise, not just panels.

Solar systems are only as reliable as the electrical work and battery management behind them. That's where most installers stop short — it's where we start.

Battery systems

LiFePO4 & BESS

Cell balancing, AC impedance monitoring, BMS configuration, and thermal derating — the detail work that determines how long a battery bank actually lasts.

Electrical engineering

Licensed EEE background

System design and installation grounded in electrical and electronics engineering practice, not just panel-fitting.

Training & standards

NSQ curriculum development

We help write the national curriculum for solar PV installation and maintenance, and train installers at Ladela Educational Institute and Jobitech Abuja.
